# Exam page override

User direction: minimal, readable, with a navigator that is comfortably sized and integrated into the exam workspace.

- Use the full viewport for the exam workspace, with 24px padding/gap on desktop. Do not cap or center the scroll container: it creates large unused side gutters on wide monitors. Keep the scroll bar at the viewport edge.
- Navigator width 320px; number cells square, approximately 48px desktop and at least 44px touch targets.
- Keep five columns on desktop. For a long exam, scroll the number grid while retaining the progress and review action.
- At 900px and below, show a labeled header toggle and a full-width expandable navigator before the question. Use as many columns as fit without reducing targets below 44px.
- Keep reading between header/footer. Selecting any navigator number closes its mobile panel and focuses the corresponding question heading, even when the index stays the same.
- Neutral surfaces, no shadows or ornamental icons. Keep answer, flag and selected-question states distinct and labeled.
